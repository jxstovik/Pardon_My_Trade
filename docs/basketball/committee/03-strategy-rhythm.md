## What winning means

Separate three objectives. They can disagree:

1. Expected category record in each regular period: the sum of eight category win probabilities, alongside the eight loss/tie probabilities and projected raw statistics.
2. Qualification/seeding and playoff-round advancement. The highest seed wins a playoff tie, so securing a seed has real value; verify exact category equality/rounding and playoff score accounting before treating a displayed 4–4 as final.
3. Probability of winning the league, respecting qualification, seeding, playoffs, future schedules, and asset value.

For this league's regular season, **every category counts toward W/L/T**: a projection for five category wins and one for eight category wins are meaningfully different, even if both would win an ordinary single-result H2H matchup. Optimize expected category wins and preserve qualification/playoff value. Championship probability becomes a useful longer-term objective after full scoring and standings semantics are modeled and validated. Display the next-period category record beside it. Do not label an expected-category count as matchup/advancement probability. In playoff simulations, use the actual bracket without reseeding and award a tied result to the higher seed.

For FG%, calculate `sum(FGM) / sum(FGA)`; for FT%, calculate `sum(FTM) / sum(FTA)`. Evaluate uncertain makes **with** their attempt volumes and the league's minimum/zero-attempt rules. An efficient player on tiny volume may barely change a team rate; a high-volume poor shooter can meaningfully move it. Model correlations among scoring, threes, attempts, and percentages so a projected improvement in one category does not hide its cost elsewhere. Use PTS/REB/AST/ST/BLK/3PTM game-level counts and FGM/FGA/FTM/FTA, not percentage-only summaries.

Turnovers have no direct scoring penalty in this league. High-usage guards should not be downgraded merely for turnovers as they would be in a nine-category league. They can still have tradeoffs through FG%/FT%, playing time and other counted categories. Do not treat a nine-category ranking or a points-league ranking as this league's board.

Project underlying game-level statistics and apply category comparisons to simulated team totals. Model availability separately only when the stat/minutes forecast is conditional on playing; multiplying an already unconditional projection by play probability would double-count injury risk. School-level cancellation or elimination and shared minutes must remain correlated where appropriate.

For each candidate lineup or move, recompute the entire feasible roster schedule and report:

- Expected PTS/REB/AST/ST/BLK/3PTM and team FG%/FT%, their prediction intervals, and changes from keeping the current roster/lineup.
- Eight category win/loss/tie probabilities, their changes in **percentage points**, expected category W/L/T, and a calibration label. Show playoff advancement probability only after its rules are verified.
- Counted starts, unused legal starts, current waiver priority, pending claims/activation times, and roster/position bottlenecks. Claim counts are unlimited under the verified rules; do not invent an add budget.
- Expected value over this period, the next 14 days, and the verified fantasy-playoff window.
- Irrecoverable costs: dropped-player value, reacquisition risk, consumed waiver priority, any verified fees, and lost future options. No FAAB bids belong in this league's report.
- Source timestamps, rules version, model version, unresolved injuries, and why the move can still fail.

Until a probability model passes time-ordered validation, display relative rankings and wide scenario ranges. Do not invent an apparently precise chance of winning from an unvalidated model or incomplete opponent lineup.

## User-switchable strategy modes

The user selected **Balanced contender** initially. For the current empty/undetermined draft state, prepare a category-specific draft board with broad category coverage, realistic minutes and positional replacement value; defer an intentional punt until the roster and category tradeoffs are known. Render all applicable alternatives in the same weekly report so the user can change direction without rebuilding the system. Modes change goals and constraints; they never override legal roster rules or transaction approvals. **Category specialization is applicable here**, but a deliberate persistent punt requires a separate choice because losing the same category each regular period has a recurring standings cost.

## Automation envelope and human decisions

**Automatic from the first usable data connection:** read league state, schedules and official reports; reconcile identities; validate scoring/legality; rebuild reports; compare modes; generate conditional alternatives; measure model quality; record decisions/outcomes; and surface only deadline-relevant exceptions. Running a local analysis job is different from a submitted platform transaction.

**Conditional future automation:** roster-slot changes among already-owned players can become automatic under a selected policy after a Fantrax write path, permission scope, fresh-state checks, lock semantics, idempotency, observed success verification, and rollback/exception behavior are implemented and tested. A browser session alone is not evidence that a supported unattended integration exists. Dry-run and shadow-mode output must be honestly labeled.

**Human decisions:** select/pivot the overarching mode, protect long-term assets, approve exact claims/drops/trades and spending where required, change a persistent category punt, and resolve a source/rules conflict the system cannot verify. Every high-impact proposal should have an expiry based on its next relevant deadline and be revalidated against a new snapshot before submission. No automatic rage-drop; no hidden transaction approval buried in a strategy toggle.

A scheduled runner should persist job state, deduplicate by league/period/snapshot/action, stop the affected execution path on missing rules or stale critical state, and retry transient reads with bounded backoff. It should produce a visible failure notification when a required check cannot run before the deadline. Notifications need a configured destination and authorized delivery; generating a local alert file is not equivalent to notifying the user. Do not claim “automatic” until the scheduler is installed, an actual scheduled run succeeds, and its report/delivery path is verified.

Suggested rollout evidence:

1. Verified league brief plus normalized rules tests, including custom scoring and lock boundaries.
2. Repeatable fixture/local snapshots and legal lineup/recommendation reports.
3. Scheduled read-only runs with visible freshness/error reporting and archived output.
4. A shadow period comparing advice to actual outcomes, including missed-start counts and prevented illegal actions.
5. Low-risk lineup execution only if a legitimate write path and explicit operating policy exist; transaction approvals remain separate.

## Acceptance metrics and switching discipline

- **Operational:** no illegal lineup recommendations; no counted games imagined before activation or after elimination; no duplicate writes; no missed detected locks; no unreported stale critical input. Track actual measured misses and failures rather than reporting target perfection as achieved.
- **Forecasting:** time-split error versus a season-average/minutes baseline; coverage of prediction intervals; availability and win-probability calibration where enough data exists; performance split by new/returning/transferred players and source coverage.
- **Decision quality:** predicted useful starts, category-stat contributions and expected category W/L/T versus realized contribution; opportunity cost of drops and claim priority; missed category value attributable to controllable bench decisions; championship/standing simulations marked experimental until evaluated.
- **Human effort:** routine brief count, approval count, urgent exceptions, and minutes of attention. The target is unattended routine research with a short list of meaningful decisions.

Show `current profile/overlay`, `candidate profile/overlay`, `Δ eight category probabilities`, `Δ expected category W/L/T`, `Δ qualification/championship estimate when supported`, `cost`, `uncertainty`, and `next irreversible decision` in the same preview. Distinguish simulation sampling error from model uncertainty: more Monte Carlo draws do not fix wrong minutes or missing injury data. Require a change to survive reasonable role/availability assumptions; a noisy ranking reversal is not enough to churn the roster. Default one-period tactical overrides expire automatically, while a persistent base-mode change is saved explicitly and auditably.
