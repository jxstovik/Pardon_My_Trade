Severity definitions: **Critical** means wrong objective, unsafe action, or corrupted evaluation/state; **High** means a materially misleading recommendation or deployment claim; **Medium** means unnecessary complexity, incomplete evidence, or a measurable implementation ambiguity.

- **Accept:** automatic research, reports, source checks and proposal preparation within the authorized task. Platform execution is a distinct capability; trades, drops and waiver claims retain the repository's exact-action approval followed by separate execution boundary.
- **Accept:** phase-one work has no platform writer dependency. Unknown facts block their dependent recommendation/action, while health reports and independent engineering work continue.

## Strategy decision

Persist Balanced as selected. Accept the revised strategy member's three lasting profiles: Balanced, Interior strength and Perimeter creation. Bigs/Guards may be concise aliases if their exact profile definitions remain explicit; these are testable roster directions, not an assertion that every player at a position shares the same statistical traits. Schedule, stability, development, sprint and playoff choices are temporary overlays, with expiry where appropriate. In the regular season compare marginal gains across the eight equally weighted category results without a predetermined punt. The report should offer distinct pivot previews, each using the same immutable forecast inputs:

1. **Balanced:** expected category wins with retained future asset value and no forced category abandonment.
2. **Weekly schedule pressure:** exploit additional *counted weekly* opportunities only when the joint category effect improves the objective; consider both shooting percentages and legal activation. Unlimited claims do not imply unlimited counted starts.
3. **Quality/stability:** preserve minutes/availability and percentage contribution when starts or weekly lineup choice are binding; lower variance is not automatically better.
4. **Development:** use replaceable reserve capacity for credible emerging roles, with explicit holding cost. No IR stash is assumed.
5. **Category concentration / sprint:** tactical category targeting is evaluated by incremental category results. A persistent punt is a user choice, and a playoff sprint uses the verified seed/tie advancement rules once comparisons are supported.
6. **Playoff runway:** trade current qualification/seeding value against games that actually count March 1–14. Do not assume NCAA tournament games count or that tournament advancement is a guaranteed future game.

Show projected category totals and shooting attempts first. Add probability deltas only when the opponent data and model support them; label research probabilities. Preserve the revised category-specific preview: expected category wins, per-category W/L/T, percentage-volume exposure, waiver priority/roster costs and future-value scenarios. Qualification/championship figures stay unavailable rather than fabricated while historical or personalized inputs are missing. Treat the March 15 administrative explanation as an inference: the explicit March 14 accumulation cutoff is verified, but reconciliation must establish the relationship rather than promote the explanation into an observed fact.
